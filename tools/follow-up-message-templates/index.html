When to follow up

Most people who go quiet are busy, not saying no. A simple rhythm works: 2 days after the first message, then 4, then 7, then 14. After the fourth follow-up, send a polite “last message” and stop. Many replies come from that last one.

Three rules that get replies

  • Keep it short. Two lines beats a paragraph on a phone screen.
  • Ask one easy question. “Still looking for this?” is easier to answer than “What do you think?”.
  • Never guilt-trip. “Just checking in” works. “I have messaged you 4 times” does not.
