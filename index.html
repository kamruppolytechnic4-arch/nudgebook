Questions

Does Nudgebook send WhatsApp messages for me?

No, and that is on purpose. It opens WhatsApp with the message typed and you press send. WhatsApp bans numbers that send automated messages, so your number stays safe.

Where is my customer list stored?

Only on your own device, inside your browser. We never see it. Use “Export backup” in Setup to keep a copy or move to a new phone.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Open the app and start. If you subscribe, you get a licence key by email that unlocks it.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Open it in Chrome or Safari and choose “Add to Home screen”. It works like an app, even without internet.

What happens after the free trial?

Your leads stay safe and you can still read and export them. Adding and sending need a subscription.

How do I cancel?

Use the link in your receipt email. No calls, no questions.
