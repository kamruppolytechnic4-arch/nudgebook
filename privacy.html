Privacy

Short version: your customer list never leaves your device.

  • Your leads, notes and messages are saved only in your own browser (local storage). We have no server that receives them and we cannot see them.
  • Your licence key is sent to our payment provider (Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy) about once a day, only to check that your subscription is active. Nothing else is sent with it.
  • Payments are handled entirely by the payment provider. We never see your card details. Their privacy policy applies to the checkout.
  • WhatsApp opens on your own phone when you tap a button. Nudgebook never sends messages by itself and never reads your chats.
  • No ads, no tracking scripts, no cookies are used on this site.
  • Deleting your data: Setup → “Delete everything”, or clear this site's data in your browser.

Nudgebook is not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta.
